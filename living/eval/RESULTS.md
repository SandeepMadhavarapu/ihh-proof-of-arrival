Precision and recall here are for the **deterministic layer** with no model: a positive "reaches the queue" when every deterministic gate passes and only meaning is left for a person. Without a key, nothing is ever accepted automatically, so the full-stack false-accept count is 0 by construction; the safety claim that matters is the deterministic rejection rate above.

## S2: recall before and after

On the 48 positives that come from human-confirmed reconciliation rows:

| Method | Passes |
|---|---|
| Before: existing pipeline gate on IHH's `Field: value` label | 34 / 48 |
| Full Gate 1 on IHH's label (structured form, value by value) | 28 / 48 |
| After: full Gate 1 on the visitor-facing sentence | 41 / 48 |

The feasibility spike measured 37 of 76 golden rows accepted by the existing gate on raw page text; the fixtures here are whitespace-normalized excerpts, so the "before" row isolates the label-vs-sentence effect. Gate 1 is stricter than the existing gate (numbers, superlatives, entity window, every span checked), so recall can only rise where sentences supply the shared words labels lack.
