The Verification Desk

Twenty real claims about five immigrants. Forty-seven named sources, thirty of them linked. You examine the claim, open the evidence and record your own finding — then say which piece of evidence settled it. No timer, no score, and you may disagree with the curator as often as you like.

Fourteen of the claims are published on a real museum website today, and ten of those did not survive checking. Finding out which is the exhibit.

Open the desk How it was built

What makes this different

Every claim is sourced

No fact appears on this site without a named, linked source. Where the record is silent or disputed, we say so on the page rather than smoothing it over.

Know what you are looking at

Open the original image record from our credits page. We distinguish historical photographs from illustrations, and flag reuse information that still needs review.

Nothing tracks you

No advertising networks, no social pixels, no font loaded from someone else’s server. People who share a family history with us should not be followed around the internet for it.
