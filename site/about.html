About

“The Immigrant Hall of Honor celebrates those contributions and preserves the stories behind them.” Its recognition program, storytelling project and digital archive are, in its own words, “laying the foundation for an interactive national museum in Central Texas.”

What we are building

Two things at once. A digital archive — the inductee profiles and the contributed family stories on this site — and a physical museum.

The museum’s plans, campaign and leadership are described on the Immigrant Hall of Honor’s own website.

How we work

Three commitments, which are the reason the rest of this site looks the way it does.

  1. We show our sources. Every fact is traceable. Where the record is thin or contested, the page says so.
  2. We credit every image. Holding institution, identifier, and a link to the record; the institution’s own rights statement word for word in the media manifest, and shown in full for the worked examples.
  3. We do not track our visitors. Someone trusting us with a family history should not be followed around the internet because of it.
