Accessibility

This site is built to meet WCAG 2.2 Level AA. Here is what that means in practice, and what we have not finished.

What we have done

  • Every page has one h1 and a heading outline that runs in order.
  • Alt text is written for every catalogued image, from the catalogue record. Extended descriptions are not written yet, because no image file ships with this package to describe; the Albright page says so where the images would go.
  • Body text contrast is at least 4.5:1, large text at least 3:1. No meaning is carried by colour alone.
  • Everything works by keyboard. Focus is always visible and never hidden behind a sticky header. Interactive targets are at least 24×24 pixels.
  • The page reflows to 200% zoom and to a phone without horizontal scrolling.
  • Motion is switched off for anyone whose system asks for reduced motion.
  • The site works with JavaScript disabled. Filtering and search are enhancements; the content is all present without them.
  • No third-party scripts, no advertising networks, no web fonts loaded from another server.

Submitting a story another way

If the web form does not work for you, we will take your story by voice, on paper, or in a language other than English, and we will do the typing. Nobody should be excluded from an immigration archive by a form.

What is not finished

The site has been through several rounds of independent review of its content and code (see TESTS-RUN.md), but not an independent accessibility audit by specialists, and not testing with screen-reader users. Both of those are still planned. We would rather say that than claim a conformance level nobody has verified.
