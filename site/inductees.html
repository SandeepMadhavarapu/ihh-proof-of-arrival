The roll, in order of arrival

Showing all 10.

  1. 1847

    Levi Strauss

    Buttenheim, Bavaria → United States

    Arrived from Bavaria at eighteen. Sold cloth to miners, then patented the rivet that held it together.

    Business Profile in preparation
  2. 1849

    John Muir

    Dunbar, Scotland → United States

    Arrived from Scotland at eleven. Did not become a citizen until he was sixty-five.

    Conservation Profile in preparation
  3. 1890

    James Naismith

    Almonte, Ontario, Canada → United States

    Invented basketball in a Massachusetts gym in December 1891, to keep students busy in winter.

    Sport and education Profile in preparation
  4. 1893

    Irving Berlin

    Tolochin, Russian Empire → United States

    Arrived at five from a village in the Russian Empire. Wrote the songs the country sang back to itself.

    Music Profile in preparation
  5. 1908

    Bob Hope

    Eltham, London, England → United States

    Came through Ellis Island at four. Spent sixty years making soldiers laugh.

    Entertainment Profile in preparation
  6. 1933

    Albert Einstein

    Ulm, Germany → United States

    Left Germany in 1933 and never returned. Took the oath of citizenship in 1940.

    Science Profile in preparation
  7. 1935

    I. M. Pei

    Guangzhou, China → United States

    Came to study for a few years and stayed seventy. Put a glass pyramid in the courtyard of the Louvre.

    Architecture Profile ready
  8. 1937

    Hedy Lamarr

    Vienna, Austria → United States

    A film star who patented a frequency-hopping system for guiding torpedoes.

    Film and invention Profile ready
  9. 1948

    Madeleine Albright

    Prague, Czechoslovakia → United States

    Fled Hitler at two, Stalin at eleven. Became the first woman to lead the State Department.

    Public service Profile ready
  10. 1961

    Celia Cruz

    Havana, Cuba → United States

    Left Havana in 1960 and never went back. Became the Queen of Salsa in exile.

    Music Profile ready
