Privacy

The short version: this site sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and loads nothing from anyone else’s server.

What this site collects

Nothing automatically. No advertising networks. No social media pixels. No analytics. No web fonts fetched from a third party. Your visit is not recorded or shared.

Why that is a deliberate choice

We ask people to tell us about leaving one country for another. Some of them are recent arrivals. Some have family who are not secure. An organisation that invites that kind of trust should not hand the visit to an advertising network, and we do not.

If the live site keeps its current analytics and advertising tags, this page must say so and name them. A privacy page that does not match the tags on the page is worse than no privacy page. The audit accompanying this build lists what is currently loading.

If you were to send us a story

  • What we would keep, if the form were live, and the contact details you give us, so we can reach you about it.
  • We do not sell it, trade it, or pass it to anyone outside the organisation.
  • You keep your copyright. We hold a non-exclusive licence to publish it.
  • You can ask us to remove it at any time and we will take it off this site.
  • If you ask us to delete your contact details, we will.
