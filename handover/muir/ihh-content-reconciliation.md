## Repository correction requiring team-lead approval

**CORRECTION SUGGESTED — not applied.** In `inductees/muir/copy.md`, the opening says “In March 1868 a thirty-year-old Scot landed in San Francisco”. Proposed replacement: “In March 1868 a twenty-nine-year-old Scot landed in San Francisco”. The [NPS chronology](https://www.nps.gov/jomu/learn/education/education-john-muir-chronology.htm), read 17 October 2026, states “1838 April 21, John Muir born in Dunbar, Scotland.” and “1868 Voyage to California: landed in San Francisco, March 28”. **Inference by date arithmetic:** his thirtieth birthday was after that arrival. Approval is needed before changing the opening and regenerating public copies.

The chronology also supports the birth date/place in the facts row. Its age-at-immigration and park terminology should not be blindly propagated: the dates and the park-versus-monument distinction require separate checks. Institutional provenance does not make every line infallible.
