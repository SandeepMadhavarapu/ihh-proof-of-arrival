### “Ph.D in Physics from University of Zurich (1906)”

- **Where:** Education field, Albert Einstein inductee page, immigranthallofhonor.org. Read 2 October 2026.
- **Verdict:** Confirmed
- **Finding:** The museum is right and this exhibit was wrong. An earlier version of this page marked 1906 as an error and gave 1905 as the correction, on the authority of the Nobel biography. The University of Zurich conferred the degree on 15 January 1906. There are three separable events here — the dissertation completed in April 1905, the faculty approval in July 1905, and the conferral in January 1906 — and flattening them is what produces the disagreement.
- **Evidence:**
  - QUOTATION — The University of Zurich, on its own conferral. University of Zurich, UZH News, “Doctor Einstein” (2022) <https://www.news.uzh.ch/en/articles/news/2022/Doctor-Einstein.html>
  - PARAPHRASE — Three dates, not one. American Institute of Physics, Center for History of Physics, Einstein chronology 1905 <https://history.aip.org/exhibits/einstein/chron-1905.htm>
  - QUOTATION — What the Nobel Prize biography says instead. NobelPrize.org, “Albert Einstein – Biographical” <https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/physics/1921/einstein/biographical/>
