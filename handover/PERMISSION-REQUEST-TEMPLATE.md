# Permission request template

**Draft only. It has not been sent to anyone.** An IHH staff member fills in the placeholders and sends it
from IHH's own account, if IHH decides to ask. Holders and routes for each item are in
[`MEDIA-PERMISSIONS.md`](MEDIA-PERMISSIONS.md). Many archives have their own request form. If the
holder's page names one, use that form and paste the relevant paragraphs into it.

---

Subject: Permission request: [ITEM TITLE] for a non-commercial museum web page

Dear [NAME OR "Rights and Reproductions team"],

I am writing on behalf of the Immigrant Hall of Honor (IHH), a non-profit that tells the stories of
immigrants who shaped the United States. Our audience includes students aged 12 to 18.

We would like to feature the following item from your collection on our profile of [INDUCTEE NAME]:

- Item: [ITEM TITLE, DATE]
- Your reference: [CATALOG ID / URL]
- Proposed use: [e.g. a [DURATION] audio excerpt / a quotation of up to [N] words / an image of the document]
- Where: [PAGE URL on IHH's site], a free, non-commercial educational web page with no advertising
- How long: [e.g. ongoing, until removed]

We would credit the item as follows, or in any form you prefer:

> [CREDIT LINE, e.g. "Courtesy of [HOLDER], [COLLECTION], [ID]"]

Could you tell us:

1. whether you hold the rights to this item, or who does;
2. whether you would grant permission for the use described, and on what terms (including any fee); and
3. how to obtain a copy suitable for the web, if you can supply one.

Thank you for caring for this material and for considering our request.

Kind regards,

[STAFF NAME]
[ROLE], Immigrant Hall of Honor
[IHH CONTACT ADDRESS]

---

### Notes for staff

- Fill every [PLACEHOLDER]. Do not add claims about the item that are not in MEDIA-PERMISSIONS.md.
- For items marked public domain or unrestricted, no letter is needed. Use the credit line given in the directory.
- Do not write to private individuals (for example, the holder of the Lamarr tapes) without first going through the organisation named in the directory.
- Keep any reply with the item's row in `media-permissions.csv` (add a `permission_granted` note), so the next person can see what was agreed.
