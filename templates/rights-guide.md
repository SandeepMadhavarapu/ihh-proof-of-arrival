# Rights in one page

**Rule 1: copy the institution's rights wording exactly** into `rights_statement_verbatim`.
Put your own judgement in `our_rights_determination`. Never mix the two.

**Rule 2: a rights statement covers one item**, not the whole collection. Check each item.

| What the record says | What it means for us |
|---|---|
| National Archives "Use: Unrestricted" | Free to use. Credit NARA and give the National Archives Identifier. |
| U.S. government work (patents, State Department, White House, Congress, the Mint) | Generally public domain. Credit the agency anyway. |
| "CC0" / Smithsonian Open Access | Free to use with no conditions. Credit the museum anyway. |
| "CC BY 4.0" | Free to use **with attribution**. The credit line is a legal condition, not a courtesy. |
| "No known restrictions" / "No known copyright restrictions" | The institution does not know of a problem. That is not a guarantee. Usually fine for a non-profit; note it. |
| "Restricted – Possibly" / "Copyright status unknown" | Do not use the image. Cite the record if it is useful. |
| "Available for reproduction… see Ordering & Use" | Usually means a permission request or a fee. Cite the record; ask before using the image. |
| "All rights reserved" (newspapers, books) | Cite it and quote briefly. Do not reproduce images or long passages. |

**Credit lines** go in `required_credit` and must appear on the page next to the item. Name the
photographer as well as the institution when the record names one.

**Alt text**: at most 15 words and 125 characters, written **after looking at the image**.
The longer `long_description` is for screen-reader users and for anyone checking our work.

**Before sending a permission request**, get the team lead's approval. Requests go out in IHH's
name, not ours.
