# Where to search, by content type

What worked for the first four inductees, in the order to try it. Log every search in
`search-log.csv`, including the ones that find nothing; a dead end is a finding.

| Content type | Try first | Then | Tips that mattered |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Arrival records** (ship, visa, admission) | National Archives Catalog — search the person's name **as spelled at the time** | Statue of Liberty–Ellis Island Foundation; FamilySearch (free logins) | Pei's 139-page file is catalogued under "Pe, Ieoh Ming", the 1935 spelling. Lamarr arrived as Hedwig Kiesler/Mandl, not "Lamarr". |
| **Naturalization** | Newspaper photo archives (Calisphere / Los Angeles Public Library found Lamarr's ceremony) | USCIS Genealogy Program (paid, slow); National Archives regional branches | Most naturalization files are not online. Say so on the page rather than guessing a year. |
| **Documents of the achievement** | U.S. Patent Office full-text PDFs (`image-ppubs.uspto.gov`); State Department archive via the Internet Archive | Presidential libraries; the American Presidency Project for speeches | Old government websites are often only on the Wayback Machine. Use its CDX index to find every archived URL in a folder. |
| **Photographs** | National Archives (look for "Use: Unrestricted"); Library of Congress (Highsmith archive is free to use) | University digital libraries (UCLA's LA Times archive is CC BY 4.0); Smithsonian Open Access (CC0) | Use Wikimedia Commons only to *find* the holding institution, then cite that institution. Skip Commons images whose source is eBay or social media. |
| **Objects** | Smithsonian Open Access API (`api.si.edu`, free key or `DEMO_KEY`) | Ask the museum for images of objects with no online photo | Most objects have no online image; the record text is still a citable source. |
| **Audio and video** | National Archives; Library of Congress; C-SPAN | University oral-history collections | Real recordings only. The team decided against AI-generated voices or video (see `site/method.html`). |
| **Biography and dates** | The subject's government or professional body (State Dept Historian, Pritzker Prize, Inventors Hall of Fame) | Scholarly encyclopedias (Britannica, Jewish Women's Archive) | Popular biography sites disagree with each other; never take a date from only one of them. |

## When a site blocks you

Several sites (loc.gov, si.edu, UCLA, JWA, Britannica) block simple scripts but open fine in a
normal browser. If a page will not load for you, try a different browser or network before
logging it as a dead end.
